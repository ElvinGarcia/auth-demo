What this shows

  • Plaintext means one breach exposes every password at once, and people reuse passwords on other sites.
  • Hashing helps, but weak passwords like qwerty still fall quickly to a wordlist.
  • Even a perfectly hashed password can still be phished, guessed online, or stuffed from another site's breach. That is why the next levels add a second factor.
